# KPI accomplishment lab / 日常工作成果包装

Read only when the user wants KPI results or performance self-assessment wording. This is one use of the accomplishment material, not the default workflow. First read [Work accomplishments](work-accomplishments.md) to group raw notes and preserve personal contribution.

## Enter the assessment scenario

Reuse existing notes and purpose. Ask only for missing assessment period, actual criteria, or required format that materially changes the draft. Without criteria, use explicitly provisional contribution themes; do not invent company KPIs. Give a usable draft before listing important gaps.

## Map to actual KPI criteria

Use the user's KPI names, targets, weights, and scoring rules when supplied. Separate target from actual result. Mark each mapping as supported, partial, or unclear; a task can be relevant without proving target attainment. Do not invent weights, completion percentages, ratings, or company standards. Without scoring rules, offer evidence and wording, not an authoritative score.

Use one primary KPI per accomplishment where possible. If it supports multiple criteria, cross-reference the same evidence rather than counting the outcome twice. For shared outcomes, distinguish the team's result from the user's contribution and do not allocate an invented personal share.

Quantify only from supplied facts: counts, coverage, delivery dates, resolution times, or comparable before/after measures. Specify the unit, period, denominator, and source where relevant. An improvement rate needs a baseline and comparable measurement; “saved time” needs a credible time comparison. Never treat goals, estimates, or hypothetical avoided losses as achieved results. When numbers are absent, concrete deliverables, complexity, and corroborated feedback can make a strong statement.

## Return usable material

Honor the user's requested length and format before choosing the output structure. A batch of notes or a KPI request does not automatically require a table.

- For “简短”, “只写一段”, or a single assessment cell, first give copy-ready prose, followed by only the necessary evidence gaps outside the submission text. Omit mapping tables, repeated explanations, and expanded versions unless requested. Do not delay the draft to collect every missing detail.
- For complex assessments with multiple criteria, targets, weights, mixed attainment, or a template requiring comparison, use a compact mapping table when it makes the assessment easier to review. Include contribution/personal role, actual criterion (or explicitly provisional theme), supported result, and material gap. Still prioritize usable assessment wording and respect any explicit request for a short response.
- Keep evidence-to-add lists focused on gaps that would change the claim. Do not embed invented numbers or “[XX%]” in copy-ready prose.

Show a before/after example when it helps the user learn the framing. Explain the improvement briefly: making the handled issue, personal contribution, or deliverable explicit. Avoid jargon such as “赋能业务闭环” that conceals the work. A concise assessment-cell version is usually enough; add an expanded review paragraph or spoken version only when useful or requested.

If a KPI was missed, state the actual gap, supported contributing factors, actions taken, and next step. Do not disguise a missed target as an achievement or blame others without evidence. A failed initiative may still demonstrate useful work or learning, clearly separated from attainment.

## Example: messy notes to formal wording

The following facts and figures are fictional examples, not defaults to reuse.

**Input:** “这季度总是催三个部门交周报，合并了12期；找出5处口径不一致，和负责人确认改好了；整理了一份口径说明，发给三个部门。KPI是按时交付周报，没记每次几点交。”

**Mapping:** Reporting delivery → consolidated 12 reports; reporting quality → identified and coordinated correction of 5 discrepancies, distributed a definitions note. Counts are user-reported. The material does not establish that all reports were on time or that later discrepancies decreased. Quality work is supporting context unless the employer has a corresponding KPI.

**Copy-ready:** “本季度协调三个部门的周报收集与汇总，完成12期报告；识别并协调修正5处统计口径不一致问题，整理口径说明并分发至相关部门，支持报告信息的一致性。”

**Evidence to add:** Delivery timestamps compared with agreed deadlines, to assess the on-time-delivery KPI. The definitions note and correction records can support the quality contribution. Do not claim “按时交付率100%”“效率提升30%” or “建立公司级数据治理体系”.

**No-result input:** “每天回答同事问题，顺手写了FAQ，没人告诉我有没有用。”

**Copy-ready:** “处理同事的日常业务咨询，将常见问题及处理方法整理为FAQ并供团队查阅。” Keep adoption and time saved unconfirmed; ask about actual use only if it would materially improve the review.

## Final fidelity check

Check every result against available facts, every role against personal responsibility, and every KPI against the supplied criteria. Remove repeated outcomes, inflated verbs, and unverified benefits. Keep the evidence gaps separate from the professional text. Do not include private emotional notes, identifying customer details, or unrelated colleague information in shared materials. Saving follows the main skill's private-record rules; drafting does not authorize publication or submission.
