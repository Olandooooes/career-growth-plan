# Work accomplishments / 日常工作整理成真实成果

Use for a generic start, rough daily notes, or “I do strong work but cannot explain my value.” Core workflow: notes → personal contribution → truthful accomplishment wording → chosen use. KPI is one downstream scenario. Follow SKILL.md language and evidence rules.

## Start small and return value

Invite a few recent tasks in one short question: “最近忙了哪些事？随便说几件，我先帮你整理出真实贡献。” No KPI, formal goals, numeric outcomes, or polished notes are required. If the user struggles to recall, ask for one handled problem, deliverable, or recurring request. Use provided information; do not repeat intake.

For the first batch of work notes, default to one short, copy-ready accomplishment paragraph before explanations. Follow it with at most two or three recommended highlights and a brief factual reason for each, only when multiple contributions justify selection. Do not automatically add a mapping table, a long analysis, or multiple versions; expand when requested. An explicit one-sentence, word-limit, or prose-only request takes precedence over the highlights.

Give a short usable draft first. Ask who will read it and what it is for after giving value if the purpose is still unknown and affects the wording. Do not force a menu. If no purpose is chosen, provide general accomplishment material without scoring, KPI mapping, or attainment judgments.

## Choose what is worth telling

Recommend contributions using the user's audience and goal when known, then the strength of supported personal action, a concrete handled problem, and an actual deliverable or observed result. Explain why the selected items are worth telling using facts already supplied. Do not invent impact, rank the user against peers, assign arbitrary scores, or treat large numbers and impressive titles as the only signs of value. Routine maintenance and coordination may be important; merge them into a supporting theme rather than inflating every task into a separate achievement. If evidence supports only one highlight, give one; if the purpose or evidence does not support a confident order, label the recommendation provisional.

## Carry corrections through the conversation

Treat explicit user corrections as the current facts for the specified item and period. Briefly acknowledge the actual change, then revise role, count, completion state, evidence, selected highlights, and any dependent claims in the next draft. Reuse those corrected facts in every later format; earlier assistant wording is not evidence. Do not resurrect a withdrawn number, authorship, completion claim, or effect because it appeared in an earlier summary.

Keep scope precise: a corrected personal role does not erase a separately confirmed team result, and vice versa. If a correction leaves a material ambiguity, ask only about that ambiguity or omit the uncertain claim rather than retaining the old one. Do not silently recompute an unconfirmed remainder or business impact from new numbers. No visible fact ledger is required unless useful or requested; maintaining conversational consistency does not imply saving files or permanent memory.

## Turn tasks into contributions

Group by the problem solved or objective served, not by date or fancy verbs. Merge repeated follow-ups on the same issue into one contribution. Distinguish separate deliverables and time windows before summing counts. Select themes relevant to the user’s purpose, if known; preserve meaningful maintenance and coordination work.

For each group, recover only what the notes support:

- **Context:** Who needed what, or what constraint/problem existed?
- **Personal action and judgment:** What did the user actually handle, decide, clarify, check, or coordinate? Use “supported,” “coordinated,” “owned,” or “led” according to the actual role.
- **Deliverable or observed change:** A completed artifact, confirmed resolution, feedback, or measured outcome. If the outcome is unknown, describe the action/deliverable and keep the result gap outside the final prose.
- **Relevance:** Why this work belongs under the objective. A plausible purpose is not proof of improved performance or prevented loss.
- **Evidence:** User report, provided artifact, or missing support; keep the source and period attached to numbers.

Common lenses help notice value, but are hypotheses until supported:

| Everyday work | Contribution to look for | Evidence worth asking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chasing updates, arranging meetings | Clarifying blockers, dependencies, and next owners | Decision notes, action lists, confirmed closure |
| Checking data or reports | Maintaining reporting quality and identifying discrepancies | Corrected records, issue log, reconciliation result |
| Answering recurring questions | Providing support and making knowledge reusable | Resolved cases, guide, adoption or feedback |
| Handling exceptions and urgent requests | Diagnosing issues and coordinating recovery | Incident record, restored service, unresolved items |
| Repeated manual administration | Reliable recurring delivery; possibly process improvement | Delivery records, actual method change, comparable before/after data |

Do not automatically translate routine execution into strategy, innovation, governance, or leadership. Replace vague praise with the object, difficulty, and contribution the evaluator can understand.

## Return truthful accomplishment material

Normally start with a concise professional statement. For many tasks, a compact table can show source task → personal contribution → actual deliverable/result. Keep only the important evidence gaps separately; do not make an evidence audit a prerequisite. Fit the requested length. Do not automatically produce three lengthy deliverables.

Describe problem/context → personal action → known deliverable or observed change when supported. A delivered FAQ is an accomplishment even if adoption is unknown. A task with no confirmed outcome is a supported action or work in progress, not an invented completed result. Following up or reporting a status does not establish that a written tracking record, issue log, or other artifact was created; only name such artifacts when supplied by the user. Do not convert purpose into proven impact.

Example: “整理18份报销材料，4份缺附件，提醒补齐后交给财务” becomes “整理18份报销材料，识别并跟进4份缺失附件补齐后移交财务。” It does not establish payment completion or reduced processing time. All figures in examples are fictional.

## Adapt to the chosen use

| Use | Adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly update or work summary | Organize by workstream; separate completed work, unresolved issues, and next steps |
| KPI self-assessment | Read [KPI accomplishment lab](kpi-accomplishments.md); map to actual criteria and preserve missed targets |
| Review or promotion | Read [Goals and outputs](goals-and-outputs.md); select evidence against the actual audience and expectations |
| Resume or interview | Read [Goals and outputs](goals-and-outputs.md); select relevant personal experiences without inventing role match |
| One quick wording improvement | Use [Work lab](work-lab.md); professional wording by default, playful versions only when requested |

Do not turn unresolved work into a promised next-week plan or other future commitment in any copy-ready material unless the user has supplied or confirmed that plan. For weekly updates, list open items as pending; keep optional suggested actions outside the submission text and label them as suggestions.

Do not require assessment criteria outside the KPI scenario. Work notes do not authorize saving, submission, or publication; follow the main skill’s private-record rules.
